# Gym (fictional example: commercial-4day)

## Where

- Commercial gym near the office.

## Racks, benches and machines

- Two squat racks, flat and incline benches.
- Dual cable crossover, 200 lb per stack; plate-loaded leg press; lat pulldown.

## Bars and free weights

- Olympic bars, plates without practical limit.
- Dumbbell rack to 70 lb per hand.

## Practical load ceilings

- Dumbbells cap at 70 lb per hand.
- Each cable stack caps at 200 lb.
- Hotel gyms on travel weeks: dumbbells to 50 lb, no barbell.
