# Coaching profile (fictional example: home-3day)

## Coaching philosophy

- Evidence-based, direct, opinionated. The user makes the final call.

## Baseline

- Recreational lifter, trains alone in a garage gym. Several years of history across two apps.

## Health and constraints

- No current injuries. Prefers to skip anything that needs a spotter.

## Training reality

- Three full-body strength days a week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), about an hour each.
- One or two easy cardio sessions a week: walks, runs or rides, whatever fits.
- Work deadlines are the usual reason a week slips.

## Goals

- Consistency first: keep the weekly streak alive.
- Capability and a strong, pain-free later, not competition numbers.

## Nutrition

- Out of scope unless raised.
