# Example instances

Fictional people, for demos and as starting points. None of them is the
author. Each pair still exercises what the evals rely on: a per-hand dumbbell
cap below the combined loads the synthetic data logs (the dual-implement
caveat), cable ceilings, and a stated weekly routine.

| Example | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `home-3day` | Garage gym, three full-body days a week | The default north star (consistency first). Used by `make demo` and the synthetic data. |
| `commercial-4day` | Commercial gym, upper/lower split four days a week | A strength-progression goal and a schedule with travel. |
| `runner-2day` | Runner who lifts twice a week at a community gym | Overriding the north star: running comes first. |

Use one with `bin/coach init --example NAME`, then edit the copies in your data
directory. Never edit these in place for your own use.
