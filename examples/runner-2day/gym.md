# Gym (fictional example: runner-2day)

## Where

- Community recreation center.

## Racks, benches and machines

- One half rack, one adjustable bench.
- Single cable column, 120 lb stack.

## Bars and free weights

- Barbell, 45 lb; plates to 255 lb total.
- Dumbbells to 40 lb per hand.

## Practical load ceilings

- Dumbbells cap at 40 lb per hand.
- Cable stack caps at 120 lb.
