# Coaching profile (fictional example: runner-2day)

## Coaching philosophy

- North star override: running performance comes first. Lifting exists to keep
  running durable, so never suggest lifting that compromises a key run.

## Baseline

- Distance runner, about 40 km a week. Lifts twice a week, newer to barbells.

## Health and constraints

- Recurring calf tightness after long runs; avoid heavy calf work in race weeks.

## Training reality

- Two strength sessions a week, 40 minutes, on easy-run days.
- Long run on the weekend, intervals midweek.

## Goals

- A half marathon in the spring.
- Keep both strength sessions through race build-up; reduce load, not frequency.

## Nutrition

- Out of scope.
